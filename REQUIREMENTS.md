# Coilover: everything Robert asked for

Compiled from the whole build conversation. This is the checklist an audit
should test against. Personal project, not GPA work.

## A. Standing constraints (violations are defects, not preferences)

A1. **No em dashes anywhere.** Not in game copy, not in UI text. He also asked
    that sentences avoid needing dashes at all where possible.
A2. **He is dyslexic. NO TIMERS AND NO COUNTDOWNS ANYWHERE, by default.**
    This explicitly includes: no decaying combo meters, no results screens, no
    star ratings, no percentage-complete bars, no speed traps (a speed trap is
    a stopwatch in disguise). Anything that counts down at the player is a
    defect.
A3. **Minimal HUD.** "A little bit too busy... needs to convey only what is
    necessary and functional." Straightforward and lightweight, no excess text.
A4. **Invented vehicle names only. No manufacturer badges, no real trade
    dress.** Specifically the white/blue/red Dakar 911 livery is a tobacco
    brand's trade dress and must never be reproduced.
A5. Ship local apps with a **Launch .bat and a Desktop shortcut**. Never make
    him use a terminal.

## B. Visual style

B1. A specific contemporary low poly style. He said three times it "isn't there".
B2. **ember.lite is the 100% prime target.** Other references (goblingrafix,
    Low Poly Shorts, Sam Benson / The Rest Is Science) count only where they
    agree with and reinforce ember.lite.
B3. He likes his sunset colours and more light. Time modes / transitions are
    good. Nostalgia matters.
B4. "I do like a bit of colour."
B5. **Real textures built from photographs**, applied across the environment,
    not just the ground.
B6. Grain and pixels must read as **world and fog texture, not a dirty screen**.
B7. More grain at dusk specifically.
B8. **2004 to 2009 visual references**, but NOT the cheesy elements. No lens
    flare, no dirty lens. What he does want is **chunkier texture filtering**.
B9. **Light sources must actually cast light** (headlights, signs, etc).
B10. Nostalgic: "so it feels like I'm just playing my friends in the early 2000s".
B11. Creative Commons images used for billboards, plane livery and other
     objects, turned into textures.

## C. Vehicle

C1. Better vehicle model; the old one was "off".
C2. **Vehicle options**, in the spirit of Dakar racers, a 911 Dakar, a Lambo
    Sterrato. (Invented equivalents, per A4.)
C3. **Super chunky tyres.**
C4. A better **360 degree vehicle view**.
C5. No gaps in the model visible from the side.
C6. Exaggerated, visible shock strut movement (the premise of the game).

## D. Driving and physics

D1. Snappier. It felt like "martian gravity" and was slow.
D2. Turning at speed felt "kind of weird".
D3. **Ground surfaces**: sand, deep sand, rock, dried riverbed.
D4. Fun arcade feel but with rigorous enough physics for surprise.

## E. Sound

E1. **Meatier engine.** It did not sound engine-like.
E2. **Rally style pops** on overrun.
E3. A better sound pack overall.

## F. Controls

F1. Analogue pedals: tap and hold, slide up for more throttle, slide down for
    more brake, release lets go.
F2. The **brake gauge must fill downward** as you pull down.
F3. **One handed mode**: like one pedal mode, but the same finger also steers
    on the X axis.
F4. The one handed steering origin must **not drift**.
F5. **100% on-press arcade mode** must remain selectable.
F6. Control options must live in a **settings menu**.

## G. Gameplay and progression

G1. A points mechanism like Forza Horizon's open world: **big air, long drifts,
    top speed, stringing gates together**, with **cool names** for the best of
    them.
G2. **Strava-like segments with leaderboards** (personal bests per named
    stretch).
G3. Progression that evolves over time; open world puttering must also be
    viable.
G4. A **running tally** so he can see how he is doing as points accumulate.
G5. Named feats worth going out of the way for.

## H. Maps

H1. More maps.
H2. An **urban stage**: "a little Toronto feeling but also a bit like san fran
    with its hills".
H3. The urban map must include **a nice flat area for huge drifts**.
H4. The city must be **fundamentally a real urban environment**, not a pattern.
H5. City terrain must be **flat, not rough**.
H6. **Buildings generated as assets and placed**, not stitched into the terrain.

## I. Process

I1. Agents should do audits, then converge on a correction and implementation
    plan.
I2. He wants **honest** assessment, including of whether the brief is met.
